How Security & Access Works

Your Ecom portal is a private, secure environment strictly for your approved customers.

How do my customers get access?

You have complete control over who sees your catalog. Our Business ERP features a strict approval flow—customers simply register on your Ecom app using their registered mobile number. The system matches this with your ERP records, and you can approve them with one click. No random public users can browse your wholesale prices, company details, or place orders.

Do I need to manage stock in both places?

Not at all! When you add a product, update stock, or generate a bill in your Business ERP, your Ecom portal updates instantly in real-time. There is zero double-entry required. If an item runs out of stock in your physical shop, it automatically disappears from the Ecom app.

How are online orders processed?

When a customer places an order on your Ecom app, it syncs instantly back to your Business ERP. Say goodbye to messy WhatsApp photo sharing and manual stock checking! You simply review and approve the order directly inside your ERP.
