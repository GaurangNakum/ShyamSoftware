Seamless Ecom Integration

Take your retail or wholesale business online 24/7.

How does the order sync work?

When an approved customer places an order in your Ecom portal, it drops directly into your Business ERP instantly. This completely eliminates data mismatch, messy WhatsApp photo sharing, and manual stock checking mistakes. Just review and approve the order with one click in the ERP to generate a Sales Order (SO). You receive 24/7 orders without having to answer the phone, and there is zero manual entry required to process them.

Do I need to manage stock twice?

No! Your Business ERP and Ecom portal are completely connected. When you sell an item at your physical counter via the ERP, it instantly deducts from the Ecom stock. Your online customers will never accidentally order an out-of-stock item.
