How to Get Ring Info
By Scanning a Ring image

ProductScan is a simple and easy web app designed specifically for the jewelry industry. Extract detailed, perfectly accurate information just by taking a photo of your designs.

  • Image to Data Magic: Simply snap or upload a picture and our app instantly extracts exact jewelry specs, measurements, and structural details.
  • Scan Raw Wax Rings: Unlike traditional tools, our app perfectly reads and recognizes details directly from raw wax rings with perfect accuracy.
  • Automatic Categorization: The app automatically categorizes your jewelry and builds an organized catalog out of your unstructured images.
  • Cloud Web App: Access your scanning tools and saved design data from any phone or computer browser, anywhere, anytime.
